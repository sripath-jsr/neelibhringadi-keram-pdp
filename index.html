Information

Go deeper.

Oil base: Sesame oil (Sesamum indicum), Coconut oil (Cocos nucifera)

Primary herbs: Neeli (Indigofera tinctoria), Bhringaraja (Eclipta alba), Amla (Emblica officinalis)

Triple-milk base: Coconut milk, Goat milk, Cow milk — a genuine differentiator in Ayurvedic hair oil formulation

Supporting herbs: Manjishtha (Rubia cordifolia), Yashtimadhu (Glycyrrhiza glabra), Haritaki (Terminalia chebula), Vibhitaki (Terminalia bellirica), Jatamansi (Nardostachys jatamansi), Padmaka (Prunus cerasoides), Sariva (Hemidesmus indicus), Nilotpala, Durva, Gunjamoola, Priyangu, Anjana, and Lauha bhasma.

Processed over 11 days using traditional Taila Paka Vidhi (classical oil preparation method).

Each batch follows the Sahasrayogam's Taila Paka Vidhi — a classical method requiring 11 days of slow-heat extraction. Herbs are first made into a kashaya (decoction), then combined with the sesame oil base and kalka (paste). The mixture is heated on low flame, monitored by hand, until the water content fully evaporates and the herbal essence binds to the oil. This process cannot be rushed or mechanized.

When will I see results?
Reduced hair fall is typically noticed within 2–3 weeks. Visible improvement in growth and texture requires 2+ months of consistent use. Recovery is a process, not a single event.

Does it help with greying?
Yes. Neeli and Bhringaraja are traditionally used to help minimise premature greying and support natural hair colour.

Can I use it on coloured hair?
Yes. The formula is gentle. It may slightly darken hair over extended use due to the Bhringaraja and Neeli content.

Is it safe during pregnancy?
Consult your Ayurvedic practitioner or doctor before use during pregnancy or nursing. The formula is for external use only.
